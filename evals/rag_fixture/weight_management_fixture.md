## 摘录 1

- Data indicate that Americans eat and drink too much added sugar. How much people consume varies by age, race, and ethnicity.
- Consuming too much added sugar can contribute to health problems such as weight gain and obesity, type 2 diabetes, and heart disease.
- The Dietary Guidelines for Americans has recommendations for limiting added sugar intake across the lifespan.
## About added sugars

## 摘录 2

To live healthier, longer lives, most Americans need to move more and eat better. This includes consuming fewer added sugars . Consuming too much added sugar can contribute to health problems such as weight gain and obesity, type 2 diabetes, and heart disease.

## 摘录 3

Consider why you want to lose weight, whether it's because heart disease runs in your family or you want to be more physically active. Writing down your reasons will help you stay focused on your goal. Place your reasons where you can see them daily to remind yourself why you want to make this change.

## 摘录 4

A lifestyle with good nutrition, regular physical activity, stress management, and enough sleep supports a healthy weight. People who lose weight at a gradual, steady pace—about 1 to 2 pounds a week—are more likely to keep the weight off than people who lose weight quicker.

## 摘录 5

- If you want to lose weight, making a specific plan can help.
- Healthy weight loss includes a lifestyle with healthy eating patterns, regular physical activity, enough sleep, and stress management.
- The five steps on this page can help you get started.
## Overview

## 摘录 6

You will need a high amount of physical activity unless you also adjust your eating patterns and reduce the amount of calories you're eating and drinking. Healthy eating combined with regular physical activity help you get to—and stay at—a healthy weight.

## 摘录 7

Both eating patterns and physical activity routines play critical roles in weight management. You can gain weight when you consume more calories than the amount of calories you burn.

## 摘录 8

Lifting weights is an example of a muscle-strengthening activity. Muscle strengthening is important for older adults who experience reduced muscle mass and muscle strength with aging. Slowly increasing the amount of weight and number of repetitions as part of muscle-strengthening activities will give you even more benefits, no matter your age.
